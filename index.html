Optical Physics Breakdown

Real Liquid Glass vs. Fake Glassmorphism

Traditional web glassmorphism is just a flat grey box with a blur filter. Apple’s macOS Tahoe & iOS 26 Liquid Glass treats UI as an active physical optical medium.

Fake Glassmorphism

2020 Web Trend
Flat semi-transparent rectangle. No refraction, no edge dispersion, no caustics, static light angle.
  • Zero background light distortion (pixels are just blurred)
  • Harsh 1px solid white border lines with digital pinching
  • Ignores pointer position and physical surface normals
  • No chromatic wavelength separation at corners

Real Liquid Glass

macOS Tahoe • iOS 26
Physical ray optics: Snell's Law ($n \approx 1.52$), edge-only chromatic aberration, and dynamic Fresnel highlights.
  • Snell's Law Refraction: Background elements bend organically beneath convex bevels
  • Edge-Only Chromatic Aberration: Prismatic cyan/magenta color split concentrated at the rim
  • Dynamic Specular Meniscus: Glare shifts with pointer angle and device coordinates
  • Mathematical Squircles: Continuous $n=4$ superellipse curves prevent digital pinching
