// Engineering Manifesto

Clean-Room Legal & Architectural Standards

Vpple operates under strict clean-room reverse-engineering methodologies. We do not extract, decompile, or redistribute proprietary Apple Inc. binaries. Every glyph curve, tracking constant, and layout metric is independently measured and rebuilt on permissively licensed open foundations.

Zero Binary Extraction

Source outline curves originate from permissively licensed open typefaces (Inter Display by Rasmus Andersson, SIL OFL 1.1). No proprietary glyph outlines or Apple TrueType instructions are bundled.

Zero Line-Wrap Delta

Mathematical normalization aligns every advance width and vertical metric to the 2048 UPM master grid. Swapping SF Pro for SP Pro Display yields 0.00px line displacement across any layout container.

Royalty-Free Freedom

Typefaces are released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; software and UI tokens are licensed under MIT. You are free to bundle, embed, modify, and distribute in commercial software without royalties.
