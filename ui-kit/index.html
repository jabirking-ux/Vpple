Physical Optical Refraction

Real Liquid Glass vs. Fake Glassmorphism

Fake glassmorphism uses static flat opacity. Real Liquid Glass refracts light according to physical optics (Snell's Law), producing chromatic aberration along the perimeter and volumetric fluid caustics. Drag the liquid lens below over the graphic spectrum and text to observe real distortion.
